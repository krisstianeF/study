City Opens New Riverside Park After Years of Planning

Residents celebrate a green space designed for walking, play, and community events

By Jordan Lee

The long-awaited Riverside Park opened to the public this morning, drawing hundreds of visitors who braved an early chill to walk the new paths and see the waterfront views. The project began more than five years ago as a plan to turn an unused stretch of riverbank into a shared space for the whole city.

Local officials said the park includes playgrounds, a small outdoor stage, and nearly two miles of walking trails. Community groups have already scheduled weekend markets and evening concerts, and nearby businesses are hoping the new attraction will bring more foot traffic to the area.
