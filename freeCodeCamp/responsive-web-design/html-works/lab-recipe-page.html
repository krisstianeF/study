Chocolate Chip Cookies

Delicious homemade chocolate chip cookies that are soft in the middle and slightly crispy on the edges.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all‑purpose flour
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • ½ cup butter, softened
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Mix flour, sugar, and butter in a large bowl.
  3. Add egg and vanilla, mix well.
  4. Stir in chocolate chips.
  5. Drop spoonfuls of dough onto a baking sheet.
  6. Bake for 10–12 minutes until golden brown.
