The Daily Grid

City Riverfront Reopens After Two-Year Restoration

After two years of construction, the city's central riverfront reopened this weekend with new walking paths, a floating stage, and nearly a thousand newly planted trees. Officials estimate that more than forty thousand residents visited on the first day alone.

Transit Ridership Hits a Decade High

Public transit recorded its busiest quarter in ten years, driven by extended evening service and a new fare program for students. Transit officials say they are now reviewing capacity on the busiest lines.

Library Extends Weekend Hours

The central library will stay open until 9 p.m. on Saturdays starting next month, following a successful six-month pilot program.

Farmers Market Moves Indoors

As temperatures drop, the weekly farmers market will move into the old train depot, keeping the same vendors and schedule.

Local Team Advances to Finals

A late goal sealed the win on Sunday, sending the city's team to its first championship final in twelve years.
